SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Master Supplier SLA defines the committed on-time delivery (OTD) performance
targets for all contracted suppliers providing materials to our manufacturing
plants across UAE, India, and Germany. Failure to meet the committed OTD target
for two consecutive quarters triggers a Corrective Action Plan (CAP) and potential
contract renegotiation.

SECTION 3: ESCALATION PROTOCOL

Level 1 (Warning): Supplier misses OTD target by 1-3 percentage points in a quarter.
  Action: Written notice to supplier account manager.

Level 2 (Corrective Action): Supplier misses OTD target by >3 points OR misses
  target for 2 consecutive quarters.
  Action: Mandatory root cause analysis within 10 business days. Corrective Action
  Plan (CAP) submitted within 30 days.

Level 3 (Contract Review): Supplier fails to meet CAP milestones OR misses target
  for 3 consecutive quarters.
  Action: Contract renegotiation or alternate supplier sourcing initiated.

SECTION 4: MEASUREMENT AND REPORTING

OTD performance is calculated monthly using data from the SUPPLY_CHAIN_DB system.
Reports are distributed to Procurement, Logistics, and Executive stakeholders on
the 5th business day of each month. The canonical formula is:

  Supplier OTD % = COUNT(POs where receipt_date <= promised_date)
                   / COUNT(All closed POs with receipt_date IS NOT NULL) * 100

This formula aligns with the governed semantic model deployed in the enterprise
Cortex Analyst system to ensure consistency across all reporting surfaces.
